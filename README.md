Midnight Vault is a complete top-down heist game: you are a thief crossing a guarded bank vault, cracking every safe, then reaching the getaway exit unseen. Three levels (The Meridian Bank, The Gilded Gallery, The Diamond Vault), patrolling guards with raycast vision cones, wall-mounted security cameras with sweeping pink cones, blinking laser tripwires that trip the alarm when touched, vault cabinets and walls for cover, shadows to hide in, spotlights that expose you, an alarm meter that fills when a guard, camera or laser sees you and triggers a chase, coin distractions, a sneak mode, an EMP gadget (E, two charges per vault) that blinds every camera for six seconds, and smoke bombs (Q, two per vault) that hide you from every guard and camera for five seconds. Loot is not grabbed on touch: stand still beside a safe for 1.4 seconds to crack it, and being spotted resets the crack.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, click to throw a coin and lure a guard away, press E to fire the EMP and Q to drop a smoke bomb. Stand still beside a safe to crack it — crack every loot to unlock the getaway exit, and time the laser beams: they blink off for a short window in each cycle.
